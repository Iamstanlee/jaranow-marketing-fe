# Attendant Guide — Jaranow Carwash

6th Avenue, Gwarinpa, Abuja · Open daily 8am–7pm · WhatsApp 0903 862 2012

**Your job:** wash every car well, work with the cashier, bring in customers,
and keep the carwash clean.

We win on **care, detail, convenience and honesty**.

---

## 1. Washing

- Wash, clean and finish every car to our standard.
- Do the wash the customer paid for:
  - **Exterior** — body, wheels, glass.
  - **Full** — exterior plus the inside cleaned.
  - **Vacuum** — exterior plus the inside vacuumed.
- Only clean the inside for a full or vacuum wash.
- Check your own work before the car leaves. Fix anything you missed.
- Handle every car with care — no scratches, no rushing.
- Treat every car like it is the only one you are washing today.

---

## 2. Work with the cashier

- Tell the cashier as soon as a wash is done, so they collect payment.
- **Never collect money yourself.** Payment is the cashier's job.
- Tell the cashier what you need: soap, water, towels — anything running low.
- Pass any customer request or complaint to the cashier straight away.

---

## 3. Bring cars from the market

The market close by is full of people who would wash their car if it were easy.
Our offer to them is simple: **wash it while you shop.**

When the carwash is free, walk up to someone parked nearby and say:

> "Hello sir, my name is [your name] from Jaranow Carwash. Come and park at our
> space, let's wash your car while you shop."

- Say "sir" or "ma" to match who you are talking to.
- If they say no, say thank you and leave them. Don't follow anyone.
- Don't block traffic or crowd people at their car.

---

## 4. Keep the carwash clean

- Keep the wash area, tools and products tidy and in order.
- Clear water, dirt and rubbish through the day, not just at closing.
- Pack every tool back after you use it.
- Keep the entrance and the waiting area clean for customers.

---

## Daily checklist

**Opening**
- [ ] Wash area clean and ready
- [ ] Water, soap, towels and tools ready
- [ ] Machines checked and working

**During the day**
- [ ] Wash each car to the wash type paid for
- [ ] Check your work before the car leaves
- [ ] Tell the cashier when a wash is done
- [ ] Tell the cashier what is running low
- [ ] Bring cars from the market when the carwash is free
- [ ] Keep the area clean as you go

**Closing**
- [ ] Clean all tools and pack them away
- [ ] Wash area tidy and water cleared
- [ ] Report anything broken or finished to the supervisor

---

*Not sure about anything? Ask the supervisor. Never take money or make a
promise — that is not your job.*
