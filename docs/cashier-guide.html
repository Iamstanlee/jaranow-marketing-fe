Customers & payment

Every customer

  • Greet everyone with a smile.
  • Ask which wash they want.
  • Tell them what the wash includes.

Prices — say it plain

WashPriceIncludes
Exterior₦2,000Body, wheels, glass
Full₦3,000Inside cleaned too
Vacuum₦4,000Inside vacuumed too
  • Same price for everybody. Don’t bargain.
  • If they beg, explain what they get. Don’t drop the price.
  • Never quote a price that is not on this list. If unsure, ask the supervisor.

Collect payment (after the wash)

  • First make sure the customer is happy. Fix anything they point out.
  • Take payment only by POS or transfer to the Jaranow bank account.
  • Give the account card so they can transfer. Never accept any other means.
  • Cash is the only exception — if the customer pays cash, accept it and record it.
  • Check the money enters before the car leaves.
  • Say thank you.

Loyalty card

  • 5 washes, then the 6th wash is free.
  • Stamp one box for each wash (boxes 1–5).
  • Write every stamp in the ledger by card number (LOY‑001, LOY‑002…).
  • The ledger is the real record, not the card. If a card is lost, the ledger still knows how many washes they’ve had.
  • When they get the free 6th wash, mark it in the ledger and give a new card.
2

Keep records

  • Write down every wash, payment and expense at once. Don’t wait till closing.
  • Keep all receipts.
  • For each wash, note: time, wash type, and the loyalty card number if they have one.
3

End of day — send to supervisor on WhatsApp

  • How many washes (by type).
  • Money expected vs money received — they must match.
  • Expenses and cash left.
  • Loyalty stamps and free washes given.
  • Cars you brought from the market.
  • Anything else worth knowing — a complaint, a repeat customer, something we ran out of.

If the money does not balance, say so. An honest report beats a tidy wrong one.

4

Bring cars from the market

The market close by is full of people who would wash their car if it were easy. Our offer to them is simple: wash it while you shop.

When the carwash is free, walk up to someone parked nearby and say:

Say this

“Hello sir, my name is [your name] from Jaranow Carwash. Come and park at our space, let’s wash your car while you shop.”

  • Say “sir” or “ma” to match who you are talking to.
  • If they say no, say thank you and leave them. Don’t follow anyone.
  • Don’t block traffic or crowd people at their car.
