Employee Handbook

The work rules for everyone employed at the wash. Read it, ask your supervisor about anything that is not clear, and sign the last page.

1

General conduct

  1. 1.1

    The work tee‑shirt is worn at all times during work hours, 8:00am to 7:00pm.

  2. 1.2

    Employees are to be on the work premises throughout work hours.

  3. 1.3

    Tell your supervisor and get their permission before leaving the premises during work hours.

  4. 1.4

    Stay calm and speak politely at all times when dealing with a customer, including when the customer does not.

  5. 1.5

    Never negotiate a price with a customer. Prices are fixed and published on the price list.

    A customer who asks for a discount is referred to the supervisor or the cashier. It is not your decision to make, and taking it out of your hands is what protects you.

2

Equipment and premises

  1. 2.1

    All work equipment is cleaned properly at the start of every business day, before the first car.

  2. 2.2

    The premises are swept and cleaned at the start of every business day, and kept clean throughout the day by the employee on cleaning duty.

  3. 2.3

    All equipment, tools and machines are used correctly, maintained, and returned to their proper storage after use.

3

Offences and sanctions

Each offence carries the sanction stated against it.

  1. 3.1

    Stealing; failing to remit a payment into the company account; or collecting a customer’s payment through a personal account.

    Immediate termination
  2. 3.2

    Quarrelling, fighting, or harassment of any kind, whether with a colleague or a customer.

    Warning, then termination
  3. 3.3

    Using company equipment or materials — fuel, detergent and the like — outside of work, without the supervisor’s approval.

    ₦5,000
  4. 3.4

    Asking a customer for a tip.

    ₦5,000
  5. 3.5

    Smoking within the work premises.

    ₦5,000
  6. 3.6

    Failing to clean the premises, or to keep them clean, when on cleaning duty.

    ₦5,000
3.7  How a sanction is applied

Every fine or warning is issued by the supervisor, recorded in writing, and the reason given to the employee at the time it is issued.

Before a sanction is recorded, the employee is told what they are accused of and given the chance to explain.

4

Revenue sharing

These are jobs worked by employees, and the money from them is shared among employees.

  1. 4.1

    Rug washing. The revenue from every rug washed goes to the employees.

    The detergent and fuel used on the rugs are paid for out of that revenue, not by the company. The split below applies to what is left after those costs.

    80%Attendants
    20%Cashier
  2. 4.2

    Motorbike washing. Employees keep 100% of the revenue from every motorbike washed. Turns are taken on the daily rotation.

  3. 4.3

    Polish. Employees may offer polish to a customer as a paid add‑on, on the daily rotation, and keep the revenue from it.

    Polish is not sold as an add‑on on the premium wash service — the company already includes it there.
