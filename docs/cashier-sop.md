# Cashier Guide — Jaranow Carwash

6th Avenue, Gwarinpa, Abuja · Open daily 8am–7pm · WhatsApp 0903 862 2012

**Your job:** take care of the customer, take the money cleanly, and keep an
honest record of the day.

We win on **care, detail, convenience and honesty**.

---

## 1. Customers & payment

### Every customer
- Greet everyone with a smile.
- Ask which wash they want.
- Tell them what the wash includes.

### Prices — say it plain
| Wash | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Exterior | ₦2,000 | Body, wheels, glass |
| Full | ₦3,000 | Inside cleaned too |
| Vacuum | ₦4,000 | Inside vacuumed too |

- Same price for everybody. Don't bargain.
- If they beg, explain what they get. Don't drop the price.
- Never quote a price that is not on this list. If unsure, ask the supervisor.

### Collect payment (after the wash)
- First make sure the customer is happy. Fix anything they point out.
- Take payment only by **POS** or **transfer to the Jaranow bank account**.
- Give the account card so they can transfer. Never accept any other means.
- Cash is the only exception — if the customer pays cash, accept it and record it.
- Check the money enters before the car leaves.
- Say thank you.

### Loyalty card
- 5 washes, then the 6th wash is free.
- Stamp one box for each wash (boxes 1–5).
- Write every stamp in the ledger by card number (JC-001, JC-002…).
- The ledger is the real record, not the card. If a card is lost, the ledger
  still knows how many washes they've had.
- When they get the free 6th wash, mark it in the ledger and give a new card.

---

## 2. Keep records

- Write down every wash, payment and expense at once. Don't wait till closing.
- Keep all receipts.

For each wash, note: time, wash type, and the loyalty card number if they have one.

---

## 3. End of day — send to supervisor on WhatsApp

- How many washes (by type).
- Money expected vs money received — they must match.
- Expenses and cash left.
- Loyalty stamps and free washes given.
- Cars you brought from the market.
- Anything else worth knowing — a complaint, a repeat customer, something we
  ran out of.

If the money does not balance, say so. An honest report beats a tidy wrong one.

---

## 4. Bring cars from the market

The market close by is full of people who would wash their car if it were easy.
Our offer to them is simple: **wash it while you shop.**

When the carwash is free, walk up to someone parked nearby and say:

> "Hello sir, my name is [your name] from Jaranow Carwash. Come and park at our
> space, let's wash your car while you shop."

- Say "sir" or "ma" to match who you are talking to.
- If they say no, say thank you and leave them. Don't follow anyone.
- Don't block traffic or crowd people at their car.

## Daily checklist

**Opening**
- [ ] Desk ready: account cards, loyalty cards, pen, the ledger, POS device
- [ ] Yesterday's report sent and money balanced
- [ ] Carwash and desk clean

**During the day**
- [ ] Greet every customer, state prices plainly
- [ ] Take payment after each wash, check it landed, say thank you
- [ ] Stamp and log every loyalty card by its number
- [ ] Write down every wash, payment and expense as it happens
- [ ] Talk to people at the market when the carwash is free

**Closing**
- [ ] Balance the money against the washes logged
- [ ] Send the end-of-day report to the supervisor
- [ ] Desk tidy, ledger, POS and cards put away and kept safe
